Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers this website. The website belongs to Local Lead Keeper, operated by Connor Haas, Virginia ("we", "us"). Contact: connor.localleadkeeper@gmail.com.

The short version

  • This site has no cookies, analytics, trackers, ads, forms, accounts or payments.
  • We only get the information you choose to send us by email or phone.
  • We use it only to reply to you and to talk with you about our service.
  • We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it for anyone's marketing.
  • Mobile numbers and text-message consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

What this website does not do

This site is three plain pages. It sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party scripts. Our font is stored on the site itself, so loading a page does not contact a font company. There are no forms, accounts, logins or payments.

What our web host logs

GitHub, Inc. hosts this site on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. These logs are GitHub's, not ours, and we do not use them. GitHub handles them under its own privacy statement: docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

What you may send us

The site has a "Book a call" button. It can open Gmail or Outlook in your browser, open your own email app, or copy our address. Gmail and Outlook are run by Google and Microsoft under their own privacy policies. Nothing reaches us until you send it.

If you contact us, we get only what you choose to share. That can be your name, email address, phone number, business name and message. Like any email, it passes through your email provider and ours.

How we use it

We use it only to:

  • reply to you; and
  • talk with you about our service, if you want that.

We reply by email, or by phone if you ask us to call you. We do not send you marketing text messages. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we stop.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with anyone for their marketing. It is seen only by:

  • us;
  • the email and phone companies that carry and store our messages and calls; and
  • anyone the law requires us to give it to, for example under a court order.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages only as long as we need them to talk with you about our service. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Our text-back service is separate

We set up missed-call text-back for local service businesses. It runs in each client's own phone and texting account, under the client's own name. It is covered by our separate client agreement and by each client's own privacy policy and texting terms. It is not covered by this policy.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has contacted us, tell us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us. Email connor.localleadkeeper@gmail.com. We keep only what the law requires us to keep.

Please do not send sensitive details

Please do not send passwords, card numbers, bank details or Social Security numbers by email or phone message. No website or email is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
