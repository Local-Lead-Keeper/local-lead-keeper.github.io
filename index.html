Questions owners ask

Something else on your mind? Just ask on the call.

How long does setup take?

My setup work takes a few days. Then the phone carriers need about 1 to 2 weeks to approve business texting in your company's name. That approval is required for business texting in the US, and I help you with the paperwork. Your Google profile work can start right away.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's one flat monthly fee, month to month, under a short written agreement. You can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. Your Google profile, your phone number and your texting account stay yours: if you leave, you just remove my access. I'll tell you the exact price on our call, and in writing before you sign anything.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. I set it up and run it for you. You'll change one call-forwarding setting on your phone, and I'll walk you through it step by step.

What do my customers see?

If you can't answer, the caller hears a short greeting in your business name that asks them to press 1 if they'd like a text. If they press 1, they get one text, for example: "Sorry we missed you, this is [your company]. How can we help? Reply STOP to opt out." They can still leave a voicemail as usual.

What about STOP and privacy?

Texts only go to people who called you and asked for one. Never marketing, only 8 am to 9 pm, and at most one text per caller per day. Anyone who replies STOP gets one confirmation and no more texts. Callers' numbers are never sold or shared for marketing.

Can you promise me more jobs?

No, and nobody honestly can. What I can promise is that I set it up properly, and that your monthly report shows exactly what was done.
