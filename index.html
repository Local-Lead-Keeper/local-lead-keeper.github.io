Missed-call text-back

Many people who reach voicemail don't leave a message. They call the next company. With the text-back, a caller you can't answer can ask for a text from your business, and you get the details on your phone.

You're busy with a customer

Your hands are full and the phone keeps ringing.

It's after hours

Calls come in at dinner time and on weekends. The caller can still press 1: their text goes out automatically when texting hours start the next morning, and you get the missed-call alert right away.

Texts go out only during texting hours (8 AM to 9 PM your time, or shorter where your state requires). After hours, the text waits until morning.

You're on another call

The second caller hears ringing, hangs up and tries someone else.

How it works

  1. 1

    A call goes unanswered

    You keep your number. Calls you can't answer roll over to a texting line set up in your business name.

  2. 2

    The caller asks for a text

    A short greeting asks them to press 1 if they'd like a text. If they do, one friendly text goes out from your business.

  3. 3

    You take it from there

    Their reply and the call details come straight to your phone. Call or text them back when you're free.

Google listing & reviews

Your Google listing and your reviews, looked after every month. Your Google listing is the box with your hours, photos and reviews that people see on Google Search and Maps. Google calls it your "Google Business Profile".

Why it matters

When people need a local business fast, they search Google and often call one of the first few they see. A listing with current hours, real photos, recent posts and replies to reviews looks active and trustworthy.

What I do every month

  • Keep your hours (including holidays), services and photos current
  • About 4 short posts a month: seasonal tips, offers, holiday hours. You approve them first
  • Draft a polite reply to every new review. Replies to bad reviews wait for your OK before they go up
  • Check that your phone number, website and service area are still right

Your part: say yes (or ask for changes) to the posts and to any reply to a bad review. That's it.

What I never do

  • Fake, bought or swapped reviews
  • Write reviews for you or your customers
  • Promise you a higher ranking

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Just ask on the call.

How long does setup take?

My setup work takes a few days. Then the phone carriers need about 1 to 2 weeks (sometimes longer, and I keep you posted) to approve business texting in your company's name. That approval is required for business texting in the US, and I help you with the paperwork. Your Google profile work can start right away.

Is there a long contract?

No. Price is one flat monthly fee, month to month, under a short written agreement. You can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. I'll tell you the exact number on a 10-minute call, and in writing before you sign anything. Texting costs (about $20 once to register, then about $20 a month in usage) are billed on your own texting account with Twilio, the texting company, at cost, with no markup. I set that account up with you. Your Google profile, your phone number and that texting line in your business name stay yours: if you leave, you just remove my access.

Do I have to give you my Google password?

No. You add me as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, and you can remove me any time. You stay the owner of your listing.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. I set it up and run it for you. You'll turn on forwarding for calls you don't answer, either on your phone or through your phone company or phone system, and I'll walk you through it step by step.

What do my customers see?

If you can't answer, the caller hears a short greeting in your business name that asks them to press 1 if they'd like a text. If they press 1, they get one text, for example: "Sorry we missed you, this is [your company]. How can we help? Reply STOP to opt out." If they call late in the evening, the text goes out the next morning when texting hours start. They can always leave a voicemail as usual.

What about STOP and privacy?

Texts only go to people who called you and asked for one. Never marketing, only during texting hours (8 AM to 9 PM your time, or shorter where your state requires; after hours, the text waits until morning), and at most one text per caller per day. Anyone who replies STOP gets one confirmation and no more texts. Callers' numbers are never sold or shared for marketing.

I'm not in Virginia. Can you still help?

Yes. Everything is set up remotely, by phone and email. Some states have stricter texting rules than federal law, so before we start I check your state's rules and adjust the setup if needed.

Can you promise me more jobs?

No, and nobody honestly can. What I can promise is that I set it up properly, and that your monthly report shows exactly what was done.
