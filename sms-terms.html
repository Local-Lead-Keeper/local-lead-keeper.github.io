Texting Terms

Last updated November 12, 2026

These terms cover the text messages sent from the Local Lead Keeper phone line. The line belongs to Local Lead Keeper, operated by Connor Haas, Virginia ("we", "us"). Contact: connor.localleadkeeper@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We text you only if you call our phone number, nobody answers, and you press 1.
  • You get one text per missed call, and no more than one per 24 hours.
  • No marketing or promotional texts.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

What this program is

Local Lead Keeper helps local businesses with three things: missed-call text-back, Google listing upkeep and a monthly report. This page covers only the text messages sent from our own phone line. We also use this line to show business owners how text-back works. If you call it and no one picks up, you can choose to get one text from us about your call. The text tells you who we are and asks how we can help.

How people opt in (for carriers)

People opt in by pressing a key on their phone during the call. There is no other way to opt in. We do not text anyone who has not pressed 1.

When you call our number and nobody answers, you hear this recorded greeting:

"Thanks for calling Local Lead Keeper. Sorry we missed your call. To get one text from us at this number about your call, press 1 now. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Or stay on the line to leave a message."

If you call outside texting hours, the greeting says when the text will come:

"Thanks for calling Local Lead Keeper. Sorry we missed your call. To get one text from us at this number about your call after 8 AM, press 1 now. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Or stay on the line to leave a message."
  • If you press 1, you hear "Thank you. We will text you at this number." and can then leave a message. Outside texting hours you hear when the text will come.
  • If you do not press 1, you get no text. You can leave a voicemail as usual.
  • Pressing 1 is your choice. You do not need to agree to texts to leave a message.
  • If we cannot text you (for example, you already got a text in the last 24 hours, or you opted out), you hear "Thank you. Please leave a message after the tone." and get no text.
  • Texts cannot reach landlines. If you press 1 from a landline, no text is sent.

Example of the text you get:

"Sorry we missed you, this is Local Lead Keeper. How can we help? Reply STOP to opt out."

How often you get texts

One text for each missed call where you pressed 1, and never more than one per caller in 24 hours. If you text us back, we may reply to your own message. If you never press 1, we never text you. Message frequency depends on how often you call and press 1.

When we text

We send the text only between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM, Eastern Time (Virginia time, where we are based). If you press 1 outside those hours, the text waits and goes out at 8:00 AM Eastern Time.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge you for texts you send and receive. We do not charge you for these texts.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any text from us. These words also work: UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, STOPALL, REVOKE and OPTOUT. You get one text confirming you are unsubscribed, and then nothing more unless you start again. You can also tell us by email or phone, and we will add your number to our do-not-text list.

How to start again

Reply START or UNSTOP to the number that texted you. You get one text confirming it. You may then get a text from us the next time you call, we miss you, and you press 1.

Help

Reply HELP to the number that texted you, or email connor.localleadkeeper@gmail.com. The HELP reply gives our name, how to reach us, the cost note and how to stop.

When you text us back

Your reply goes to the owner of Local Lead Keeper. We may call you or text you back from a different phone number.

No marketing, no sharing

We send no marketing or promotional texts from this line. We do not sell phone numbers. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we handle your information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

Delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If it is urgent, please call us again or email us.

Client businesses

These terms are for our own phone line only. When we set up text-back for a client business, it runs on that business's own number and in its name, under that business's own privacy policy and texting terms.

Changes

If we change these terms, we post the new version on this page with a new date. We will not start sending a different kind of text under these terms.
